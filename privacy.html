Anonymous visit analytics

This portfolio automatically records anonymous visit analytics without a pop-up: time when the page is visible, projects visible on screen, video playback while visible, and clicks on CV and contact buttons. It also records the application-link label (for example “sienna”), referral website hostname, approximate country and city provided by the hosting platform, and a broad device/browser category.

A random identifier groups pages within the same tab session. It is not a persistent identifier for tracking repeat visitors across days. It does not provide your real name, email address, or employer. A company link identifies the link used, not who opened it. The portfolio does not save raw IP addresses in its analytics records; a temporary server hash is used for rate limiting.

Central records expire after 90 days. Only the owner can access them using the protected dashboard. Visible time does not prove attention or reading, and the last update is not a confirmed departure time. Browser blocking or connection loss may prevent recording. Recording starts when the configured service loads; no previous visits are reconstructed.

You can disable or re-enable this tracker here. This preference is stored locally in this browser, and previous opt-outs remain respected; the site's existing basic Vercel Web Analytics is separate. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected by the detailed tracker.
