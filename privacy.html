Privacy & analytics

Detailed analytics are OFF by default. They start only after you actively choose “Allow analytics”. Rejecting analytics does not restrict access to the portfolio.

Purpose

If you opt in, this portfolio uses privacy-minimized first-party analytics only to understand portfolio usage and improve presentation. The analytics are not used for advertising, behavioural profiling, sale of data, credit decisions, or identifying individual visitors.

What may be recorded after consent

  • country supplied by the hosting platform, not city or precise location;
  • broad device and browser category;
  • referral website hostname and an application-link label when a specially labelled portfolio link is used;
  • time while the page is visible, projects visible on screen, visible video playback, and clicks on CV or contact links.

What is not recorded by detailed analytics

The detailed analytics do not intentionally record your name, email address, employer, precise location, raw IP address, advertising identifiers, or a persistent identifier designed to follow you across days or unrelated websites. A random identifier groups pages only within the same browser-tab session. A company-labelled link shows which link was opened; it does not prove who opened it.

Retention and access

Detailed central analytics are retained for up to 30 days and are available only through the owner's password-protected dashboard. A short-lived keyed hash of an IP address may be used only to rate-limit abusive requests; the raw IP address is not stored in the analytics record.

Your choice

You may allow or reject detailed analytics below. There is no penalty for rejecting. Your choice is stored locally so the site can remember it. You may change your choice later on this page. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are treated as a rejection signal for the detailed tracker.

International privacy approach

This portfolio follows a conservative privacy-by-default approach: data minimization, opt-in before detailed analytics, equal reject controls, short retention, no advertising use, no sale of analytics data, and respect for browser privacy signals. Privacy laws differ by country and can depend on the operator, visitors, hosting, transfers, age, and future site features, so no website can truthfully promise automatic compliance with every law worldwide without ongoing legal review.

Important limits

Visible time is an estimate and does not prove that a visitor was actively reading. The final update is not guaranteed to be the exact departure time. Browser privacy tools, blocked requests, or connection loss may prevent some visits from appearing.
