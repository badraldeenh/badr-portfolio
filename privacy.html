Anonymous visit analytics

With your permission, this portfolio records time when the page is visible, projects visible on screen, video playback while visible, and clicks on CV and contact buttons. It also records the application-link label (for example “sienna”), referral website hostname, approximate country and city provided by the hosting platform, and a broad device/browser category.

A random browser identifier helps count repeat visits. It does not provide your real name, email address, or employer. A company link identifies the link used, not who opened it. The portfolio does not save raw IP addresses in its analytics records; a temporary server hash is used for rate limiting.

Central records expire after 90 days. Only the owner can access them using the protected dashboard. Visible time does not prove attention or reading, and the last update is not a confirmed departure time. Browser blocking or connection loss may prevent recording. Data starts after permission is granted; no previous visits are reconstructed.

Your choice is stored locally on this browser. Declining disables this detailed tracker; the site's existing basic Vercel Web Analytics is separate. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected by the detailed tracker.
