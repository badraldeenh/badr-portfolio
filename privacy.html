Privacy & analytics

This portfolio does not use detailed per-visitor session tracking. There is no analytics consent pop-up. Privacy-minimized aggregate analytics may be used to understand overall traffic and improve the portfolio.

What is not collected by the portfolio's custom analytics

The custom detailed tracker is disabled. The portfolio does not intentionally create a visitor profile, record a named visitor's journey, store precise location, or measure a specific visitor's time across projects.

Aggregate analytics

The hosting platform may provide first-party aggregate web analytics such as page views, general country, browser/device category, referral source and requested page. These analytics are used only to understand overall portfolio performance and are not used for advertising, behavioural profiling or sale of visitor data.

Data minimization

  • No advertising trackers are intentionally installed.
  • No custom persistent visitor identifier is used.
  • No custom raw IP address is stored in portfolio analytics records.
  • No city or precise geolocation is collected by the custom analytics system.
  • No attempt is made to identify a visitor by name, email address or employer from analytics data.

International privacy approach

The portfolio follows a data-minimization and privacy-by-design approach intended to reduce privacy risk across jurisdictions. Privacy requirements differ by country and can change over time, so this notice does not claim guaranteed legal compliance in every jurisdiction.
