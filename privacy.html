Privacy & anonymous analytics

This portfolio uses privacy-minimized first-party analytics to understand whether the site and its projects are useful to visitors. The analytics are not used for advertising, profiling, sale of data, or identifying individual visitors.

What may be recorded

  • the country supplied by the hosting platform, not city or precise location;
  • broad device and browser category;
  • the referral website hostname and an application-link label when a specially labelled portfolio link is used;
  • time while the page is visible, projects visible on screen, visible video playback, and clicks on CV or contact links.

What is not recorded

The detailed analytics do not intentionally record your name, email address, employer, precise location, raw IP address, advertising identifiers, or a persistent identifier designed to follow you across days or unrelated websites. A random identifier only groups pages within the same browser tab session. A company-labelled link shows which link was opened; it does not prove who opened it.

Retention and access

Detailed central analytics are retained for up to 30 days and are available only through the owner's password-protected dashboard. A short-lived keyed hash of an IP address may be used only to rate-limit abusive requests; the raw IP address is not stored in the analytics record.

Your controls

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are respected by the detailed tracker. You can also disable or re-enable detailed anonymous analytics in this browser below. The preference itself is stored locally so the site can remember your choice.

Important limits

Visible time is an estimate and does not prove that a visitor was actively reading. The final update is not guaranteed to be the exact departure time. Browser privacy tools, blocked requests, or connection loss may prevent some visits from appearing.
