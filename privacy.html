Privacy

This portfolio does not use visitor analytics or detailed session tracking. The only custom measurement retained is a simple count when someone clicks the CV PDF link.

CV click counter

When the CV link is clicked, the site increments an aggregate counter. The counter is used only to understand how often the CV link is opened. It does not store the visitor's browsing history, page-view history, country, device, browser, name, email address, employer, or precise location.

What is not collected

  • No custom page-view analytics.
  • No session duration tracking.
  • No project or video viewing history.
  • No custom persistent visitor identifier.
  • No custom raw IP address stored in CV counter records.
  • No city or precise geolocation.
  • No advertising or behavioural profiling trackers intentionally installed.

Data minimization

The CV counter stores only aggregate counts by day and a last-click timestamp for the private owner dashboard. It is not intended to identify individual visitors.
