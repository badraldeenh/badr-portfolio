Privacy

This portfolio uses Vercel Web Analytics and limited first-party visit analytics to understand traffic and improve the portfolio.

Visit analytics

Visit records use a random identifier for the current browsing session. They include country, general device and browser type, referral source, pages viewed, visible browsing time, project and video engagement, and link clicks. Presence indicates recent activity in a visible tab, not confirmed identity. Records expire after 30 days. Names, emails, raw IP addresses and precise locations are not stored in these visit records.

Your choice

First-party measurement respects Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track and a saved opt-out. You can stop future analytics collection in this browser below.

CV clicks

A separate aggregate counter records CV-link clicks by day. It does not identify visitors.
