01 • Project Overview

A controlled system, not a one-off mockup.

The goal was to build a portfolio project that reads like a real packaging assignment: clear positioning, a disciplined identity, flavor architecture, shelf logic and campaign applications. The four core flavors remain fixed throughout the case study.

Brand Idea

Crispy by Nature.

KETTLO combines rustic food cues with a sharper contemporary layout. The visual language avoids overly decorative packaging and instead relies on a bold wordmark, confident blocks of flavor color and simple potato-chip imagery.

Workflow disclosure: this is a self-initiated portfolio concept. AI-assisted visualization was used during concept exploration and mockup development; the final case-study structure, flavor system and presentation are intentionally controlled and disclosed rather than presented as commissioned client work.

One wordmark. One architecture. Four flavor signals.

03 • Packaging Architecture

Flavor changes. Brand recognition stays.

Every SKU keeps the same hierarchy: brand at the top, product descriptor below, flavor in the middle and a strong food zone at the base. Color does the fast recognition work while layout remains constant.

01 • POSITION

Natural, bold, contemporary

Define the balance between rustic food character and a cleaner modern shelf presence.

02 • IDENTITY

Reduce to one strong wordmark

Keep the mark highly visible and avoid decorative elements that compete with the food.

03 • SYSTEM

Build the four-flavor architecture

Lock the hierarchy first; let color and ingredient cues create flavor differentiation.

04 • APPLICATION

Test beyond the pack

Extend the same system into social, retail and outdoor applications to verify consistency.

05 • Brand Applications

The same idea at every scale.

The campaign system uses the dark green master color, the large wordmark and product-color accents so the brand remains recognizable in fast environments such as supermarket aisles, mobile feeds and outdoor advertising.
