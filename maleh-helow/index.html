01 • Project Brief

A premium campaign system across different product categories.

The seven-post collection brings chocolates, kunafa, traditional sweets, cake and savoury products into one recognisable campaign language. The case study focuses on consistency, product appeal and a polished social-media presentation.

The work

One visual language, seven different products.

The campaign uses an ivory-led palette, restrained decorative details and elegant black accents to create continuity while allowing each product to remain the focus. The result is a flexible system that can move between gift, dessert and savoury content without losing brand consistency.

02 • Creative Direction

Designed to feel premium without overpowering the food.

The strongest part of the system is restraint. Decorative elements support the product instead of competing with it, while typography and framing keep the campaign coherent from post to post.

01

Product Appetite

Large, clear product imagery keeps texture, filling and presentation at the centre of every composition.

02

Campaign Consistency

Ivory backgrounds, floral details and dark ribbon accents create a recognisable visual family.

03

Editorial Balance

Spacing and restrained styling give the posts a polished, premium feel suited to gifting and confectionery.

03 • Featured Pairing

From chocolate gifting to traditional dessert.

These two pieces show how the same campaign language adapts to products with very different shapes, textures and visual weight.

Campaign principle

Consistency without repetition.

The post retains the same premium framing and campaign cues while changing the composition around the product itself. This makes the system recognisable without forcing every post into the same layout.
