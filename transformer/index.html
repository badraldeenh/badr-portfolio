Commercial Case Study • Automotive Advertising

Transformer General Trading

Automotive social media design built around product clarity, bold promotional hierarchy and campaign-ready visuals for off-road and vehicle accessories.

Social Media DesignProduct AdvertisingAdobe Photoshop8 Selected Designs
01 • Project Brief

Commercial visuals designed for fast product recognition.

This selected campaign work demonstrates how individual automotive products can be translated into clear, high-impact social content. The portfolio selection focuses on readable product presentation, promotional hierarchy and a consistent visual standard across different brands and categories.

The work

From product information to campaign-ready social graphics.

The collection spans new arrivals, Ramadan promotions, off-road equipment, automotive electronics and outdoor products. Each layout keeps the product central while balancing brand assets, offer messaging and vehicle or lifestyle context.

02 • Creative Direction

A visual system built around product, offer and context.

Rather than treating the eight posts as isolated artwork, this case study groups them by the design decisions repeated across the campaign: product dominance, concise messaging and category-specific atmosphere.

01

Product First

Large product imagery and controlled composition keep the advertised item immediately identifiable.

02

Promotional Hierarchy

Headlines, campaign labels and offer messaging are separated clearly from supporting visual detail.

03

Automotive Context

Vehicles, terrain and outdoor environments give each product an appropriate real-world use context.

03 • Featured Campaign

Seasonal promotion with a strong product focus.

The Ramadan pieces extend the same core system into seasonal advertising while preserving the automotive character of the campaign.

Campaign principle

Seasonal styling without losing product clarity.

The promotional treatment adds a Ramadan context while the product and vehicle remain the primary visual anchors. This keeps the design commercially useful rather than decorative.

04 • Selected Work

Eight campaign visuals across multiple automotive categories.

The remaining pieces show how the same advertising discipline adapts to different brands, formats and product types.
